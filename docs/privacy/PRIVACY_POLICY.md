# Privacy Policy — Check Printer & Check Writer

**Effective date:** September 19, 2026  
**Provider:** WorksBien Studios Inc. (“WorksBien”, “we”, “us”)

This policy applies to the Windows application **Check Printer & Check Writer** (the “App”).

## The short version

The App is local-first. It does not require a WorksBien account and does not contain WorksBien cloud sync, analytics, telemetry, advertising or behavioural tracking. Cheque records are stored in encrypted form on the Windows device unless the user chooses to export, print or back them up.

## Information handled by the App

To perform its core function, the App may process information that the user enters or imports, including:

- account nicknames, payor or company names and addresses;
- payee names and addresses;
- cheque numbers, dates, amounts, memos, categories and cheque status;
- printer, paper-stock and calibration identifiers; and
- encrypted-backup metadata.

The App does not ask for online-banking credentials, account passwords, PINs or payment-card details. It is designed for compatible **preprinted cheque stock** and does not need bank routing or account numbers to print its supported variable fields.

## Where information is stored

Live App data is stored locally in encrypted form. The live encryption key is protected using Windows facilities. Portable backups are encrypted with a separate passphrase chosen by the user. WorksBien does not receive that passphrase and cannot recover it.

The user controls backup and export destinations. Files placed in OneDrive, Dropbox, a network folder, email, removable media or another third-party service are then handled under that provider’s terms and privacy practices. The App does not automatically upload those files to WorksBien.

## Printing and PDF export

When the user saves a PDF, the App writes it to the location the user selects. When the user prints, Windows sends the document to the printer, print driver or virtual printer selected by the user. Those local or third-party components may process document contents as necessary to complete the user’s request. WorksBien does not receive the document from the App.

## Microsoft Store purchases

Microsoft processes the purchase, payment, tax, account and refund information associated with Microsoft Store transactions. The App requests a Store-localized price and license status for its `lifetime_unlock` add-on. The App does not receive the user’s full payment-card details or a general purchase history. Microsoft’s handling is governed by Microsoft’s own terms and privacy statement.

The App may retain protected local metadata showing that Microsoft previously verified Lifetime access and when it was last verified. This permits a limited offline grace period; it is not used for tracking or advertising.

## Information WorksBien receives

The App does not automatically send cheque data or usage analytics to WorksBien. If a user voluntarily contacts support, WorksBien receives the information the user includes in that communication. The in-App “Copy diagnostics” function is designed to exclude payee names, cheque amounts, bank details and addresses, but users should still review diagnostic text before sending it.

## Purposes

The App processes local information only to create and validate cheque PDFs, operate printing, maintain the local register, protect cheque numbering, restore local data and verify Store entitlement. WorksBien does not sell App data or use it for advertising.

## Retention and user controls

Local records remain until the user archives or deletes eligible records, removes the App data, restores a different backup, or uninstalls and removes its data. Historical printed snapshots and audit records are intentionally retained inside the local data file to preserve register integrity. Users may export their register, create an encrypted backup, delete eligible drafts and payees, archive accounts/payees, or remove all local App data using Windows.

Store transaction retention is controlled by Microsoft. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary to answer the request, prevent abuse, comply with law and resolve disputes.

## Security

The App uses authenticated encryption for its local data and verified encrypted backups. No system can eliminate every risk. Users are responsible for securing their Windows account, physical cheque stock, exported PDFs, backup passphrases, printers and backup destinations.

## Children

The App is a business utility and is not directed to children. WorksBien does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the App.

## Changes

We will update this policy when App data practices materially change. The effective date will be revised, and material changes will be disclosed through the App, Store listing or WorksBien website as appropriate.
